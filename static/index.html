CT spleen volumetry

Connecting to API

Upload an abdominal CT volume and get the spleen volume in mL. A 3D U-Net built with MONAI, served with FastAPI.

Research demo, not a medical device. Do not upload real patient data. The model segments the spleen only, was trained on 41 public scans (MSD Task09), and has no separate test set.
